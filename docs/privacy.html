Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  PawRecord v1.0

PawRecord ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

PawRecord stores all pet health data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers.

  • Pet profiles: Name, species, breed, date of birth, photos — stored locally only.
  • Health records: Vaccinations, medications, weight logs, vet visits — stored locally in SQLite on your device.
  • Subscription data: Processed by RevenueCat. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Photos: Accessed only when you explicitly add a photo. Never uploaded to any server.
  • App preferences: Language, weight unit, notification settings — stored locally.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell your data to any third parties.
  • We do not use analytics that identify individual users.
  • We do not run servers that receive your pet health data.

3. iCloud Backup (Premium Feature)

If you enable iCloud backup (Premium only), your data is synced via Apple's iCloud service. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store and managed by RevenueCat. We receive only an anonymous subscriber ID — no payment card details, no personal billing information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Push Notifications

Local push notifications are generated on-device for vaccination and medication reminders. Notification content never leaves your device. We request notification permission only when you set up a reminder.

6. Camera & Photo Library

We request camera and photo library access only when you explicitly add a pet photo. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

7. Children's Privacy

PawRecord is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time via Settings → Delete All Data within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

For subscription-related data managed by RevenueCat, contact them directly at revenuecat.com/privacy.

9. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • Apple iCloud — Optional backup (Premium)
  • Apple App Store — Payment processing

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: support@pawrecord.app
